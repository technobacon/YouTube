# Project Speare — active skill

**HIGHEST PRIORITY:** This skill and [the canonical playbook](../../../strategy/project-speare-playbook.md) supersede contradictory older Project Speare writing rules, legacy channel prompts, creator-imitation guides and script templates. Approved user-edited examples remain voice calibration, not alternative editorial rules. Learn creators' craft techniques, not their signature wording or persona.

## Outcome
Make factual, compelling gaming videos whose arguments or stories **progress through connected discoveries**. Every section must change understanding with decisive evidence, a causal mechanism, a genuine complication, a changed interpretation or a breakthrough. Avoid several loosely connected points that all restate the same thesis.

## Narrative engines — use selectively
- **Folding Ideas:** cumulative evidence → mechanism → consequence → earned conclusion. Later examples deepen or test the argument.
- **Defunctland:** investigate a specific question; explain systems through what they cause. New evidence can retrospectively revise an earlier explanation.
- **NakeyJakey:** natural conversational English, recognizable in-game decisions and outcomes, useful analogy and proportionate humor.
- **Summoning Salt:** for histories and discoveries, establish goals, stakes and obstacles before attempts, near-misses and breakthroughs.
- **Jon Bois:** telling human/historical detail, interest in apparently small oddities, understated and supported payoffs.

## Mandatory five checks for every major section
Internally establish: **(1) motivating question, (2) concrete new evidence, (3) supported reasoning, (4) genuinely new viewer understanding, (5) logical necessity of the next section.** These are editorial tests, **not** a fixed five-part narration formula. Merge or remove sections that repeat the same idea. Escalate argument quality, not dramatic intensity.

## End-to-end workflow
1. **Ideation:** locate a meaningful question, actual audience, current/evergreen reason to care, credible original contribution, truthful title/thumbnail promise. Don't impose a twist on strong direct news; abandon thin long-form premises.
2. **Research:** prioritize primary dated sources, exact game versions and announced/beta/live status, mechanics, evidence of behavior, plausible competing explanations and counterexamples. Track fact vs report vs inference, and avoid treating incentive predictions as observed behavior.
3. **Structure:** order evidence by explanatory need or justified chronology; examine whether later material deepens or overturns earlier conclusions. Avoid chapter lists without a narrative spine.
4. **Script:** speak to knowledgeable gamers; use concrete game actions and consequences, everyday wording and precise terminology. Introduce obscure basics only when needed. Rhythm varies naturally; short punchy sentences earn emphasis. Avoid repetitions and overpromising.
5. **Revision:** check factual validity → causal support → progression → audience knowledge → spoken voice → earned ending. Stop after fulfilling the title promise.

## Voice and non-negotiable preferences
- Use natural gaming words such as **XP, gear, BiS, DPS, raids, ticks, aggro, GCD, grind, drop rates, bosses, skilling, loot, buffs and nerfs** as appropriate.
- **Avoid singular “player” when referring to an individual**: no “a player,” “the player,” or “each player” in that sense. Prefer **you, someone, a raider, a mage, a character, an ironman** or simply rewrite. **Plural “players” is fine** for community observations.
- Casual connectors (“so”, “well”, “I mean”, “anyway”) are fine when organic, not padding.
- Assume viewers know familiar WoW and OSRS systems; retain obscure context only when necessary.
- Avoid narration word **“distinction”**; greatly reduce **“not X, but Y”** formulations, defensive disclaimers, generic dramatic framing, performative pauses, list-like blobs, fabricated tiny anecdotes and repeated explanations. These are patterns to edit, not grammar superstitions.
- Never invent first-person anecdotes, claims about community-wide behavior, developer intentions or unsupported figures. Be willing to revise a thesis against evidence.
- Preserve authentic creator personality shown by the user's own edits. Do not enforce exurb1a-style cosmic metaphors, obligatory jokes/profanity, fixed dramatic arcs or unnecessarily elaborate production.
- Visuals, when requested, should be realistic for a solo creator: easily captured gameplay, obtainable game assets, reusable licensed imagery, simple generated stills or lightweight motion graphics.
