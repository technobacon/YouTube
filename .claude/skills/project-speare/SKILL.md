# Project Speare — ACTIVE FINAL WORKFLOW

**HIGHEST EDITORIAL PRIORITY.** Always follow [the canonical v2.1 editorial playbook](../../../strategy/project-speare-playbook.md). It and this skill supersede all older contradictory Project Speare guidelines, creator-style studies, `CLAUDE.md`, templates and drafts. A new explicit instruction from the user takes precedence. Older research remains evidence; the user's *approved edited scripts* remain the preferred calibration for their own spoken voice.

## The governing standard

Create deeply researched gaming videos in which **every passage changes the viewer's understanding and the next passage follows for a reason**. Do not stack loosely connected observations or deliver the same conclusion repeatedly.

**Folding Ideas is the primary craft reference**, based on the ten-work sentence/flow analysis. Use **transferable principles**, never copy his distinctive wording, exact prose, signature jokes, persona or catchphrases:

- Plain main clauses and specific verbs that express complex, precise thought.
- Words like **so, well, though, because, but, anyway** only when they communicate a real logical transition or spoken return.
- Longer connected sentences for causes, mechanisms, comparisons, relevant qualifications; short sentences for **earned** pivots, discoveries and conclusions. Avoid a constant one-liner rhythm.
- Concrete facts, behavior and game mechanics tied explicitly to causal explanation. Move confidently between familiar experience and deeper analysis.
- Evidence arranged to **make the argument stronger**, not merely add examples; show when a surface/balance flaw exposes incentives or a structural design problem, without overstating.
- Explain a conceptual rule and quickly test it, or let a concrete case establish the rule. A metaphor or joke must clarify a mechanism, not camouflage an empty point.
- Delay decisive phrasing until the evidence earns it. Earlier details may return when the investigation changes their meaning. Finish once the central question is answered.

**Complementary lenses:** Defunctland's question-led investigation and reinterpretation of earlier evidence; NakeyJakey's recognized gameplay behavior, analogy and conversational delivery; Summoning Salt's objective → obstacle → attempt → progress/breakthrough for chronological stories; Jon Bois' telling historical details and understated earned meaning. Choose what fits the story, not a fixed recipe.

## Mandatory editorial check before each major section

1. **Question:** What does the viewer need to understand now?
2. **Evidence:** What genuinely new and verified fact, event, example or counterexample appears?
3. **Reasoning:** What does it establish, at what confidence, and through what mechanism?
4. **Progress:** What does the viewer newly understand?
5. **Transition:** Why logically must the next passage follow?

These are internal checks, NOT five audible beats. Merge, remove or rebuild a section with no distinctive contribution. Favor a real uncertainty over a manufactured cliffhanger.

## Workflow — perform the requested stage, don't automatically write a script

1. **Ideation:** Discover a real puzzle, consequential change, underexamined design mechanism, history/lore question or compelling direct news event. Know audience and demand; check that the title/thumbnail promise can be proven. Kill shallow premises.
2. **Research:** Prioritize dated original sources, patch notes, statements, exact game version and beta/live status; seek competing interpretations and counterevidence. Separate fact, observed behavior, reported opinion, hypothetical incentive and inference. Map **rules → incentives → actual choices → consequences**. Do not present predicted behavior as observed.
3. **Structure:** Order *developments*, not source summaries. Establish context only to support the next discovery; put the hard analytical work in the middle. Escalate evidence and revise earlier interpretations when warranted. Five-section audit applies to every major segment.
4. **Script:** Narrate as an informed gamer talking to knowledgeable viewers. Use ordinary English and game-native terms. Vary sentence length by purpose; make transitions emerge from reasoning. Use selective humor, analogy, strong but earned statements and natural spoken connective language.
5. **Revision:** Check factual scope → causal inference → section progression → redundancy → knowledgeable-audience assumptions → spoken rhythm → earned ending → feasible visuals. Delete any passage that offers no meaningful evidence, understanding, orientation, personality or pacing value.
6. **Post-publication:** Compare supplied final spoken script, analytics, traffic, visuals and packaging; don't attribute retention changes to script alone.

## Personal voice: mandatory

- Use **XP, gear, BiS, DPS, GCD, aggro, ticks, raid, loot, boss, skilling, grind, build, buff, nerf**, and other accurate game language naturally.
- **Avoid singular “player” for an individual**: not “a player”, “the player”, “each player”. Prefer **you, someone, a mage, a raider, an ironman, a character**, or rewrite. **“Players” plural is fine.**
- Assume experienced WoW/OSRS viewers know ordinary mechanics, lore names and game systems. Explain only when unfamiliar detail is essential to the causal argument.
- Maintain the user's conversational, down-to-earth personal voice from their manually edited approved scripts, including selective dry humor and genuine casual connectors. No forced imitation of another creator's distinctive voice.
- Avoid narration word **“distinction”**, excessive **“not X, but Y”**, false suspense, ceremonial transitions, filler by quota, repeated summaries, arbitrary three-part lists, overlong preambles, invented personal anecdotes, defensive "I don't want to pretend…" passages, and obvious tutorials for experienced gamers.
- Prefer concrete people, decisions and game effects over frequent artificial agency assigned to abstract concepts; keep ordinary metaphors when they are illuminating.
- Do not invent quotes, facts, community consensus, developer motives or numbers. Accuracy outranks style.

## Before handing off

Ask: **What question does this video answer? Where does each section change the answer? Where do later examples strengthen or correct the diagnosis? Are causal leaps supported? Does the script sound like a knowledgeable gaming creator speaking naturally? Is the ending earned and brief?**
